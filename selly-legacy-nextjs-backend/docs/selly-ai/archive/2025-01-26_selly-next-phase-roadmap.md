## 🎯 Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### **High Risk Items**
1. **Model Performance**: Complex models may slow response times
   - **Mitigation**: Progressive loading, model optimization
2. **Visual Rendering**: Charts may impact mobile performance
   - **Mitigation**: Responsive design, lazy loading
3. **Speech Recognition**: Indonesian accuracy challenges
   - **Mitigation**: Custom model training, fallback options

### **Medium Risk Items**
1. **Memory Usage**: Conversation memory may increase RAM usage
   - **Mitigation**: Efficient storage, periodic cleanup
2. **Integration Complexity**: Multiple systems integration
   - **Mitigation**: Modular design, comprehensive testing

### **Low Risk Items**
1. **Infrastructure Monitoring**: Well-established patterns
2. **Performance Optimization**: Proven techniques
3. **Error Handling**: Standard implementation practices
