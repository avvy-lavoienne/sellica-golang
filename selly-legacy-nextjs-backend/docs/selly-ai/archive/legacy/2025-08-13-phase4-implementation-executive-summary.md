## ⚠️ **Risk Management and Mitigation**

### **Strategic Risk Assessment**

#### **Government Approval and Compliance (High Impact, Medium Probability)**
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Early and continuous government stakeholder engagement from Week 1
- Compliance validation integrated throughout development, not just at the end
- Legal and regulatory expertise embedded in the development team
- Automated compliance validation using enhanced Augment rules

#### **Technical Integration Complexity (Medium Impact, High Probability)**
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Bi-weekly cross-workstream integration testing
- Feature flags for gradual integration and rollback capability
- Dedicated integration environments for continuous testing
- Expert consultation for complex government system integrations

#### **Performance at National Scale (High Impact, Low Probability)**
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Continuous load testing throughout development
- Performance budgets enforced at component level
- Intelligent monitoring and alerting from day one
- Auto-scaling validation with synthetic load generation

### **Success Assurance Framework**
- **Weekly Risk Assessment**: Proactive identification and mitigation
- **Bi-weekly Stakeholder Updates**: Transparent communication with government partners
- **Monthly Success Metric Review**: Performance tracking with corrective actions
- **Quality Gates**: Enhanced Augment rules compliance at every milestone
